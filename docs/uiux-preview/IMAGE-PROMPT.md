# Ảnh minh họa bãi đỗ

Tạo bằng built-in image_gen. Bản gốc PNG 1536×1024 được sao chép vào `assets/parking-courtyard.png` để dùng trong mẫu đăng nhập. Đây là minh họa ý tưởng, không phải ảnh hay sơ đồ bãi đỗ thực tế. Không thay thế asset nào trong frontend hiện tại.

Prompt cuối cùng:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create one polished landscape editorial illustration for the login panel of an existing Vietnamese smart parking operations app, ImperiaSmart Parking. Subject: an architectural miniature of a well-organized urban parking courtyard, viewed from a gentle isometric angle, a few modern cars placed in spacious parking bays, a simple entry barrier, one discreet camera on a pole and tidy small trees. Visual language: refined architectural illustration, soft matte surfaces, crisp readable forms, restrained details, calm daylight and soft shadows. Palette matches the existing app: deep navy, muted blue, pale stone/ivory, subtle emerald green parking-bay accents, a small warm amber accent. Composition: complete centered parking miniature on an airy light ivory ground, generous breathing room around it; landscape aspect ratio roughly 3:2, suitable for a large web login illustration and responsive cropping. It is a conceptual scene, not a real site map, not a photo, not a technical dashboard. No text, no lettering, no branding, no labels, no UI widgets, no numbers, no glossy neon, no purple gradients, no dark busy background, no futuristic holograms. Attractive enough for a premium product illustration, but calm and practical rather than playful or toy-like. Generate a single cohesive image, not a contact sheet.
